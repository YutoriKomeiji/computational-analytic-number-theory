# Computational Analytic Number Theory

A public-facing home for carefully scoped computational research, reproducible verification, and independent review in analytic number theory.

## Status

**Repository initialized. No research artifact, numerical certificate, or manuscript has been released here yet.** This repository is not a claim to have proved the Riemann hypothesis (RH), generalized RH, or any other open problem.

This public repository is **separate from the private internal research workspace**. It has its own history and will contain only separately reviewed, explicitly approved public release packages. Unpublished notes, internal evidence archives, raw session logs, credentials, and private repository history are not mirrored.

## What to expect

- Source-pinned methods and precise mathematical statements, including their domains, conventions and assumptions.
- Small reproducible computational experiments and independent-checking procedures when they are cleared for public release.
- Explicit distinctions among historical run receipts, independently reproduced results, rigorous outward interval certificates, formal typechecking, mathematical proofs, and conjectures.
- Negative findings, unresolved obligations, prior-art comparisons and known limitations.

## Evidence and claim policy

A published research package must identify its source version and digest, dependencies, exact reproducer, expected output, evidence limitations, attribution, relevant licenses, and an explicit claim ceiling. A finite experiment never implies a global theorem by itself. A green Lean build is not automatically a mathematical review or evidence of RH.

**All RH/GRH claims remain unproven within this repository unless a specific, independently checkable argument is actually published and reviewed.** No such release is present today.

## Participation

Independent, technically specific reviews, reproduction reports, falsification attempts and corrections will be welcome once a concrete public package is available. Please do not post sensitive data. A separate contribution protocol will accompany the first approved research release.

## Release boundary

This initial README documents the repository's purpose and verification standards only. Publication of research results, datasets, manuscripts or contact with third-party projects remains subject to explicit owner approval of the exact content and destination. No external RH proof or participation status is asserted here.
